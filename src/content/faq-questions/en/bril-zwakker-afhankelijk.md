question: Will glasses make my child’s eyes weaker or dependent on them?
---

No. Glasses do not make your child’s eyes weaker or dependent. Some parents notice that their child seems to see less well without glasses than before. That is because the child has become used to seeing sharply, not because their eyes have got worse. Please don’t doubt the importance of the glasses, as irregular wear can hold back the treatment.
