question: Can you test my child’s eyes before they can read or speak?
---

Yes. Your child doesn’t need to talk or know letters to have their eyes examined. We use age-appropriate methods, such as watching how your child follows objects or lights, picture charts and playful tests. With eye drops, we can also measure objectively whether glasses are needed, without your child having to answer anything.
