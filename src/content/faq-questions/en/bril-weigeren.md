question: What should I do if my child refuses to wear them?
---

A few tips that can help:

- Build up wearing time gradually, for example during a fun activity.
- Encourage your child positively and praise them.
- Use role models, such as books or characters who also wear glasses.
- Make sure the frame fits well and is comfortable.

If your child keeps refusing, please contact us. Sometimes there is a reason, such as a poorly fitting frame or a prescription that needs adjusting.
