question: Why are dilating eye drops needed, and how long do their effects last?
---

The drops temporarily relax the eye’s focusing muscle (cycloplegia) and widen the pupil (dilation).

- With the eyes fully relaxed, we can reliably measure your child’s prescription. Only this measurement lets us decide whether glasses are needed.
- The wider pupil lets us examine the inside of the eye, including the retina and optic nerve.

The effect usually lasts from a few hours until the next day. During that time, your child may have blurry near vision and be sensitive to bright light. Sunglasses or a cap can help.
