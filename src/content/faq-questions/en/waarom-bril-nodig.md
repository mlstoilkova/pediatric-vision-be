question: Why does my child need glasses if they can see well?
---

Even if your child seems to see well, there may be a risk of a lazy eye, or even two lazy eyes. During the critical period when vision is developing, glasses are a treatment, not just an aid to see better. If the ophthalmologist has prescribed glasses, it is important that your child wears them consistently, even if you don’t notice anything wrong with their vision.
